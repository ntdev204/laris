it does not qualify the model, which stays NOT QUALIFIED.

- `2026-11-27/`: two topics of 18 papers each (MPC on embedded hardware; hallucination detection in RAG), baselines
  on the same 8 papers, extraction against an independent gold standard, time-split check, the G2 conflict rule
  reviewed, ranking weights, and the system's own reports as appendices (`BAO-CAO.md`, `BAO-CAO.docx`, Vietnamese).
  The screenshots and the run folders stay in the ignored `artifacts/gap-eval/`. Parts that need people (tag
  precision, a team member's gold standard, expert 1–5 ratings) are marked as not done.
