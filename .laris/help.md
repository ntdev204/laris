# Using LARIS

LARIS is a research assistant that works on your own material. It searches your documents and notes, cites what it
finds, reads files, and follows workflows for common research tasks. This text is prepared in advance and printed
as it is; no model writes it.

## Asking

Ask in plain language. For questions about your material LARIS searches first and cites each statement as
`[source #rank]`. When the evidence is missing it says so instead of guessing.

## Workflows

A workflow is a fixed procedure for one kind of task. Start one with `/workflow <name> <your request>` and list them
with `/workflows`. A workflow can only narrow what LARIS does: fewer tools, fewer steps, required sections in the
answer.

## Approvals

Anything that changes a file asks you first. A refusal is final for that action.

## Limits

The model behind LARIS is a research build and is not yet qualified. Treat answers as leads to check against the
cited sources, not as conclusions.
