# Venue checklist

## Verification sources (record URL and date)
- Publisher/journal official site: aims and scope, author guidelines, APCs, review model, AI policy.
- Indexing: the publisher's indexing statement, plus direct checks in indexing databases when accessible.
- Metrics (impact factor, CiteScore, quartile): only from the official source of that metric; note the year.
- Review time: official statistics if published; otherwise label as "author-reported, unofficial".

## Predatory / low-quality red flags
- Promises of very fast acceptance or guaranteed publication.
- Unsolicited invitation emails with flattery; editor or board not verifiable.
- Scope extremely broad; fees hidden until after acceptance.
- Fake or misleading metrics; journal name imitating a known journal.
- No clear peer-review policy, retraction policy, or archiving.
- Not indexed where it claims to be.

## Submission checklist (adapt to verified guidelines)
- Manuscript in the venue template, within limits; anonymized if double-blind.
- Title page / author info as required; ORCID if required.
- Abstract and keywords within limits.
- Figures in required formats and resolution; separate files if required.
- Cover letter; suggested/opposed reviewers if requested.
- Statements: conflicts of interest, funding, data/code availability, author contributions, AI use.
- Supplementary material; copyright/OA choice; fees approved.
