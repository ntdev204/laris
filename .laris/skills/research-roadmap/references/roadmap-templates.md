# Roadmap templates

## Milestone plan
| Milestone | Deliverable | Depends on | Effort (weeks, estimate) | Start | Due | Buffer | Status |

## Publication pipeline
| Paper | Thesis chapter | Contribution | Target venue (verified) | Stage (idea/draft/submitted/revision/accepted) | Next action | Due |

## Advisor meeting brief (1-2 pages)
1. Since last meeting (date): done, with evidence (figures, files).
2. Key results and what they mean (with limits).
3. Problems and blockers.
4. Decisions needed from the advisor (numbered, with options and my recommendation).
5. Questions (from open-questions.md).
6. Plan until next meeting.

## Progress report (semester/annual; adapt to institutional form)
Summary; objectives for the period; work completed; results; publications and submissions; deviations from
plan and reasons; plan for next period; risks; support needed.

## Capacity check
Available hours/week x weeks = capacity. Sum of estimates + 20-30% buffer must fit; otherwise cut scope.
