## Specific aims page (common structure; follow the funder's if different)
1. Context and problem (2-3 sentences). 2. Gap and its cost. 3. Long-term goal and objective of this project.
4. Central hypothesis and rationale (preliminary results). 5. Aims (2-3), each with approach and expected outcome.
6. Expected impact.

## Approach section per aim
Rationale -> approach -> expected results -> potential problems and alternative strategies -> success criteria.

## Budget justification
| Item | Cost | Quantity | Total | Justification (link to aim) | Funder rule satisfied |

## Panel-style self-critique
For each criterion: score (funder's scale), strengths, weaknesses, how to fix. Common weaknesses:
aims dependent on each other (one fails, all fail), vague methods, no alternatives, overambitious timeline,
weak preliminary data, budget not tied to aims.
