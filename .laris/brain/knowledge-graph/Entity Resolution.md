# Entity Resolution

Merge mentions of the same entity using normalised names, aliases and context; keep unmerged doubts visible.


Normalise case, spacing and punctuation. Merge on exact alias match or on a strong key such as an identifier. Mark near matches as candidates for review rather than merging them. Keep the original mentions on the node.
