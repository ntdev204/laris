# Claims and Results

A claim is what the authors assert; a result is what was measured. Keep them apart.


Claims often use hedges and verbs of assertion. Results carry numbers, conditions and comparisons. Limitations are stated as scope or caveats. Keep all three with their sentences and never merge them into one summary sentence.
