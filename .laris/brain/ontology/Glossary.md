# Glossary

Short definitions of terms used across directions: passage, corpus, claim, result, provenance, hop, coverage.


Passage: a span of a document with its source and position. Corpus: the set of ingested documents. Claim: what authors assert. Result: what was measured. Provenance: where a fact came from. Hop: one edge along a path. Coverage: the share of items that were successfully processed.
