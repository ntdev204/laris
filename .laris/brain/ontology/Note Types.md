# Note Types

Brain notes are hubs (root, domain, topic), playbooks, concepts, maps, policies and vocabularies.


A hub lists children with a purpose and a when-trigger. A playbook has Decide, Verify and Stop. A concept explains one idea. A map says which tool or workflow fits a job. A policy states a boundary. A vocabulary fixes terms.
