# Answer Quality Rubric

Score answers on grounding, completeness, abstention behaviour and format; each score cites the evidence checked.


Grounding: every claim has a supporting passage. Completeness: each sub-question is addressed. Abstention: unanswerable questions are declined with a reason. Format: required sections present. Score each separately; never average them into one number.
