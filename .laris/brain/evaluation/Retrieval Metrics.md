# Retrieval Metrics

Recall at k, MRR and nDCG on a fixed labelled fixture; report the fixture and k.


Recall at k measures coverage, MRR measures how early the first relevant passage appears, nDCG weights graded relevance by rank. Compare modes on the same fixture and queries. A metric without its fixture name and k is not reported.
