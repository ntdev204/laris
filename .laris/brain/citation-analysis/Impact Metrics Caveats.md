# Impact Metrics Caveats

Counts and indices depend on field, age and corpus; state scope and avoid ranking from thin data.


Older papers accumulate citations; normalise by age or compare within a cohort. A corpus sample undercounts citations. Self-citation and review articles distort counts. Report the metric with its denominator, never as a single score.
