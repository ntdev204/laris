# Citation Context

Keep the sentence that holds a citation; it tells whether the cited work is used, extended or criticised.


Capture the citing sentence and its neighbours. Classify only with explicit cues (for example, comparison words or limitation words) and mark the rest unknown. Do not infer sentiment from a bare number in brackets.
