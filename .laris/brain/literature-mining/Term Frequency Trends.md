# Term Frequency Trends

Count terms per document per period, normalise by period size, and report the denominator.


Use document frequency (documents mentioning the term) rather than raw counts. Normalise by the number of documents in each period. A term seen in few documents is an anecdote; say so. Do not claim a trend from fewer than three periods.
