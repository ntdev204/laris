# Question Types

Factual, comparative, causal, methodological and definitional questions need different evidence.


A factual question needs one clear passage. A comparative question needs one passage per side under the same conditions. A causal question needs evidence of mechanism or controlled comparison, otherwise say the sources only show association. A methodological question needs the methods section, not the abstract.
