# Evidence Sufficiency

Evidence suffices when a passage directly states the claim under the same conditions; otherwise abstain or qualify.


Directly stated beats inferred. Same population, method and period beats a loose match. One source is a single point of failure: say so. Weak evidence is reported as weak, not omitted. No passage means no answer, only a statement of what was searched.
