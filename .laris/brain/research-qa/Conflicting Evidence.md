# Conflicting Evidence

When sources disagree, report both positions, their conditions, and what would resolve it.


Check whether the disagreement is real or due to different definitions, populations or metrics. Prefer the source that states its conditions. Never average conflicting claims. Give the user the sources so they can judge.
